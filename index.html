Profile

Who signs this edition

Hi, I'm Laura! I live in Limeira, Brazil, and I spend my days turning products into stories people want to watch until the very end. I believe the best content is the kind that feels real, because it is real: real people using, feeling and sharing what they think.

I work with beauty, food, home decor and travel, and I have created content for hotels that wanted to show what it feels like to stay with them. I also work with brands of any kind, remotely or on location, and I film in English and Portuguese.

I film, edit and produce everything myself, with my own setup: phone, lighting, microphone and tripod. My approach is simple: I get to know your brand, write a script that sounds human and film every detail with care.

If you want content that brings your brand closer to people, we are going to get along really well.
